# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and technical; disagree with ideas, not people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who
do not follow these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the contact options
on their GitHub profile (<https://github.com/mehmetalicann>). Security vulnerabilities go through
the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.
